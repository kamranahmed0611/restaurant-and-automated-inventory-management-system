1. Executive Summary & Design Philosophy

The Muchachos Restaurant Operating System (ROS) is a resilient, hybrid point-of-sale (POS), kitchen display system (KDS), inventory management, and financial reporting platform. It was engineered to solve the most critical risk facing food businesses: network failures halting counter operations.

🖥️ Local Laptop: Sole Authoritative Master

Packaged into an Electron Windows application. Powered by Dexie.js (IndexedDB) and a standalone Next.js server running on 127.0.0.1:31415. All order ticketing, printing, and recipe calculations execute at 0ms latency and remain 100% operational offline.

☁️ Remote Cloud: Read-Only Live Mirror

Hosted on Vercel backed by Supabase (PostgreSQL). Provides the restaurant owner with real-time visibility into sales velocity, shift metrics, and P&L statements on any smartphone without exposing counter write permissions.

4. Core Functional Subsystems & Technical Details

4.1 POS Register & Order Lifecycle

  • Three Transactional Flows: Supports Dine-In (parked table tabs with bill splitting/merging), Fast-Casual / Takeaway, and Foodpanda.
  • Atomic Checkout: Orders are written directly to Dexie. Fast-casual orders enter PENDING status to trigger Kitchen Display alerts, transitioning to COMPLETED upon food preparation.
  • Thermal Slip Generation: Instant formatting of 80mm customer bills, kitchen prep tickets, and QR validation codes.

4.2 Kitchen Display System (KDS)

  • Real-Time Ticket Dispatch: New tickets appear instantaneously via posBus cross-tab notifications without network roundtrips.
  • Audio Chimes & Visual Aging: Custom Web Audio synthesis sounds when tickets arrive. Cards change color dynamically as wait times increase.
  • "Mark as Ready" Workflow: Kitchen operators mark items prepared with 1-click, completing orders and updating cashier logs immediately.

4.3 Inventory & Dynamic Recipe COGS Engine

  • Bill of Materials (BOM): Every menu item is composed of granular raw ingredients (e.g. burger patty, bun, cheese slice, sauce grams).
  • Dynamic Price Recalculation: When a manager restocks an ingredient at a new cost, the system reactively updates the Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) and profit margins across all attached menu items in real-time.
  • Automated Stock Deductions: Completing an order automatically deducts the exact recipe quantities from raw inventory with complete spoilage auditing.

4.4 Financial Auditing & Shift Settlement

  • Shift Management: Cashiers log opening cash floats and track cash drops. At shift closure, the system produces an official thermal Z-Report.
  • Foodpanda Commission Settlement: Dedicated reconciliation ledger tracking standard 30% platform commissions, pending payouts, and payout deductions.
  • Expense Tracking: Direct logging of petty cash expenses linked to the P&L ledger with automatic net profit recalculations.

4.5 Multi-Device Security & Role Separation

  • isLaptopWriter() Guard: Strict origin validation ensuring only the physical counter machine has write permissions. Remote phone users cannot delete expenses, modify recipes, or alter menu prices.
  • Master PIN Protection: Encrypted 4-digit PIN required to execute critical operations (shift audits, terminal provisioning, database resets).
